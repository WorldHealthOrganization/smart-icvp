2. ICVPMinVaccineDetails.vp
Definition

Vaccine Product ID

ShortVaccine Product ID
Comments

LOINC 39236-5; SNOMED CT 787859002.

Control1..1
BindingThe codes SHALL be taken from WHO ICVP Vaccine Product Ids
(required to http://smart.who.int/icvp/ValueSet/ICVPProductIds)
Typecode
Primitive ValueThis primitive element may be present, or absent, or replaced by an extension
Requirements

Satisfies REQ-DE-11 (ICVP.C5.DE.11) and REQ-CC-06. SHALL carry the identifier of the vaccine/prophylaxis product administered. Bound (required) to ICVPProductIds — a product outside this catalogue SHALL be rejected by ICVP.DT.1 / ICVP.DT.2. Disease (REQ-DE-12) and manufacturer (REQ-DE-16) are DERIVED from vp via the ICVP Product Catalogue and are NOT independently populated.

Definitional CodesICVP.Core CodeSystem for Data Elements: ICVP.C5.DE.11 (Vaccine or prophylaxis), LOINC: 39236-5 (Vaccine code), SNOMED CT: 787859002 (Vaccine product (medicinal product))
4. ICVPMinVaccineDetails.dv
Definition

Date of vaccination

ShortDate of vaccination
Comments

LOINC 30952-6.

Control1..1
Typedate
Primitive ValueThis primitive element may be present, or absent, or replaced by an extension
Requirements

Satisfies REQ-DE-13 (ICVP.C5.DE.13). SHALL be the calendar date on which the vaccine/prophylaxis was administered — the anchor for all temporal-validity calculations (REQ-DE-20, REQ-DE-21/22/23). SHALL NOT be later than the certificate's issuance date or the verification date; MAY precede the issuance date.

Definitional CodesICVP.Core CodeSystem for Data Elements: ICVP.C5.DE.13 (Date), LOINC: 30952-6 (Date and time of vaccination)
6. ICVPMinVaccineDetails.cn
Definition

Name of supervising clinician

ShortName of supervising clinician
Control0..1
Typestring
Primitive ValueThis primitive element may be present, or absent, or replaced by an extension
Requirements

Satisfies REQ-DE-14 (ICVP.C5.DE.14) and contributes to REQ-CC-07. MAY carry the full name of the supervising clinician. Either cn or is (REQ-DE-15) SHALL be present — see invariant must-have-issuer-or-clinician-name on DVCMinVaccineDetails. This is the digital counterpart of the Annex 6 clinician name column, NOT of the wet-ink signature (REQ-CC-08).

Definitional CodesICVP.Core CodeSystem for Data Elements: ICVP.C5.DE.14 (Name of supervising clinician)
8. ICVPMinVaccineDetails.is
Definition

Certificate issuer id (referenced organization)

ShortCertificate issuer id (referenced organization)
Control0..1
Typeid
Primitive ValueThis primitive element may be present, or absent, or replaced by an extension
Requirements

Satisfies REQ-DE-15 (ICVP.C5.DE.15) and contributes to REQ-CC-07 / REQ-CC-10. MAY carry the identifier of the relevant issuing authority; SHALL correspond to a participant in the GDHCN SMART Trust ValueSet-Participants. Either cn (REQ-DE-14) or is SHALL be present. Certificates intended for international border verification SHOULD populate is — without it, cryptographic verification through the GDHCN is not possible.

Definitional CodesICVP.Core CodeSystem for Data Elements: ICVP.C5.DE.15 (Relevant authority responsible for issuing this certificate)
10. ICVPMinVaccineDetails.bo
Definition

Batch No

ShortBatch No
Comments

LOINC 30959-1 'Lot number [Identifier] Vaccine'.

Control1..1
Typestring
Primitive ValueThis primitive element may be present, or absent, or replaced by an extension
Requirements

Satisfies REQ-DE-17 (ICVP.C5.DE.17) and absorbs REQ-DE-18 (DE.18) and REQ-DE-19 (DE.19). SHALL carry the batch (lot) number as recorded by the manufacturer. Where a coded catalogue of batches is available, populate bo with the manufacturer's canonical batch identifier to preserve recall and pharmacovigilance traceability. SHALL NOT be omitted (Coexistence footnote 28).

Definitional CodesICVP.Core CodeSystem for Data Elements: ICVP.C5.DE.17 (Batch No. of vaccine or prophylaxis), LOINC: 30959-1 (Lot number [Identifier] Vaccine)
