This page lists the principal legal, policy, and technical references underpinning this implementation guide. Standards and profiles used by the computable artifacts are listed separately on the [Dependencies](https://worldhealthorganization.github.io/smart-icvp/dependencies.html) page.

**International Health Regulations**
- [International Health Regulations (2005), as amended in 2014, 2022 and 2024](https://apps.who.int/gb/bd/pdf_files/IHR_2014-2022-2024-en.pdf)
- [Resolution WHA58.3 (2005): Revision of the International Health Regulations](https://apps.who.int/gb/ebwha/pdf_files/WHA58/WHA58_3-en.pdf)
- [Resolution WHA67.13 (2014): Implementation of the International Health Regulations (2005)](https://apps.who.int/gb/ebwha/pdf_files/wha67/a67_r13-en.pdf)
- [Resolution WHA75.12 (2022): Amendments to the International Health Regulations (2005)](https://apps.who.int/gb/ebwha/pdf_files/WHA75/A75_R12-en.pdf)
- [Resolution WHA77.17 (2024): Amendments to the International Health Regulations (2005)](https://apps.who.int/gb/ebwha/pdf_files/WHA77/A77_R17-en.pdf)

The provisions most directly relevant to this implementation guide are Article 35, Article 36, Annex 6, and Annex 7 of the IHR.
