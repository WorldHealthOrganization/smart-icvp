## Validation Logic

This page describes the Validation logic derived from the applicable IHR requirements for ICVP.

##### A. Validating an ICVP Certificate Claim

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Yellow fever vaccination | Vaccine or prophylaxis is included in WHO List of Prequalified Vaccines, the WHO Emergency use listing procedure or the Prequalified lists - Finished pharmaceutical products AND the Number of days since vaccine administered (Today’s date – “Date”) is more than or equal to 10 days |
| Poliovirus vaccination | Vaccine or prophylaxis is included in WHO List of Prequalified Vaccines, the WHO Emergency use listing procedure or the Prequalified lists - Finished pharmaceutical products |
